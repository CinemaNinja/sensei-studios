👑 MASTERPIECE MURAL • FEATURED ARTWORK

Tree>Triangle

$42,000 Exhibiting at The Grove

This mural is a sacred tribute to the enduring spirit of the Great Horned Owl. Born directly from the forest, it is crafted from 95% locally and harmoniously sourced aspen wood. The journey began by respectfully harvesting the trees and milling the timber by hand. I planed, cut, and sanded every single triangle, fitting them together like a complex, wooden puzzle. Pouring my energy into this artwork for 32 demanding days, the final piece is a reflection of both the natural beauty of the wood and the wisdom of the owl.

This piece is available for $42,000. In the spirit of reciprocity and community support, 50% of the sale will be distributed as donations to local environmental, agricultural, and creative organizations:

  • 10% to The Grove in Aspen, the communal workspace where this artwork currently lives.
  • 10% to the Aspen Center for Environmental Studies (ACES).
  • 10% to the Central Rocky Mountain Permaculture Institute (CRMPI).
  • 10% to Sustainable Settings in Carbondale, CO.
  • 10% to The Farm Collaborative.
Specs: Hand-milled forest timber, Great Horned Owl geometric mosaic

About Daniel Kelly Brown

Part Cinematographer. Part Ninja.

Daniel Kelly Brown is a cinematographer and artist based in Colorado. For twenty-five years he has blended technical mastery of robotics and 3D rendering with the instinct of an action-sports filmmaker.

“Efficiency of movement in life, and during video shoots. I practice and practice to ensure the client gets the perfect shot, every time.”

Whether skiing with a custom gimbal, setting multi-robot 8K timelapses on mountain peaks, or flying tight FPV lines through architecture — Danny operates as a complete one-man band.
