About Daniel Kelly Brown

Part Cinematographer. Part Ninja.

Daniel Kelly Brown is a cinematographer and artist based in Colorado. For twenty-five years he has blended technical mastery of robotics and 3D rendering with the instinct of an action-sports filmmaker.

“Efficiency of movement in life, and during video shoots. I practice and practice to ensure the client gets the perfect shot, every time.”

Whether skiing with a custom gimbal, setting multi-robot 8K timelapses on mountain peaks, or flying tight FPV lines through architecture — Danny operates as a complete one-man band.
