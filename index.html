06 Chapter · Vision Peace Protocol Autonomous abundance, regenerative robotics & art as civic glue
Civilization Prototype · Roaring Fork Valley

Humanity Harmonizing System

Nature, AI, & Intelligent Machines

A grassroots vision for the Roaring Fork Valley and beyond: a self-sustaining system that guarantees free food, free transportation, free shelter, and universal basic income, held together by a shift in human focus away from mere survival and toward the creation of art, where art is anything practiced with attention, imagination, and the desire to grow.

The unused symphony

We possess instruments of astonishing beauty: machines that learn, systems that move, tools that can feed, house, carry, and create. They will expand beyond what any one mind can hold. Right now we do not play them together. We play them for advantage. We play them against the grain of Mother Nature.

Peace Protocol asks a harder question than invention: how do we wrangle this technology in, gather it into one coherent practice, and use it for the benefit of all? How do we let it lift the burden of survival, so that what remains is the ability to simply play, and so that human life. Technological foundation and mother nature can keep time with each other.

A pattern, not a clone

Peace Protocol begins small. We will build it first as a working microcosm, close enough to the ground to prove the concept in living practice. If that seed takes, the hope is that the Roaring Fork Valley takes it up as its own, and that from this valley it grows.

It is not a clone to be dropped onto someone else's land. It is an ever-evolving system, meant to be transplanted, reshaped, and grown in new climates, new cultures, new towns. A pattern of abundance that can take root anywhere.

Valley Master Plan · Grassroots Pilot
The Grassroots Vision & The Human-Machine Alliance

A New Beginning: Human Craft & Robotic Kinship

The Peace Protocol begins as a hands-on local workshop in the Roaring Fork Valley. Human craftsmen work alongside supervised first-generation robotics to build fine cabinetry, timber sculpture, and community infrastructure, documenting the entire journey publicly as living proof that intelligent machines can serve human creativity rather than replace it.

100% of the revenue generated by automated production is channeled directly into a permanent community fund. This fund builds toward four guaranteed pillars of security: free food, free transportation, free shelter, and universal basic income. By erasing the daily struggle to survive, the protocol gives residents their time and attention back, redirecting human energy toward creation itself.

This blueprint unfolds at a macro scale across a dedicated 180-acre sanctuary in Old Snowmass: a physical proof ground where woodshops, greenhouses, subterranean gardens, and art temples are built in phases from real land. Rooted in transparency, craft, and planetary stewardship, it provides clean energy, living food, and regional mobility through automated infrastructure designed to make humans flourish and nature thrive.

A law older than us

Entropy

the universal tendency toward disorder

Entropy is not new. Humanity has grappled with it since the dawn of time, and always will. Yet the vision of robots building robots, repairing robots, and sustaining systems across generations is one of our most profound acts of resistance.

Graphene is a possible way to meet that law without pretending we can eliminate it. A carbon lattice that resists wear, so water, transit, and machines can be kept long enough for the next generation to keep them again.

Living Food Sanctuary · Botanical Intelligence
Foundational Food Security & Living Systems

The Virtual Garden & The Grandfather Brain

The main goal

Time-lapse every single plant, under every single growing circumstance, and feed that living record into the Grandfather Brain.

That is the work. Every species, every light, every soil, every season, every stress. Cameras stay with the plants until the Grandfather Brain has a complete picture of how life actually grows.

Around that core, the Virtual Garden is a living botanical system and time-lapse laboratory for plant health, natural light, and automated organic food production. To protect alpine ecosystems and secure year-round harvests, the garden grows in climate-secure subterranean pods deep beneath the 180-acre sanctuary in Old Snowmass, refracting natural sunlight through mirror networks.

The Grandfather Brain is a nature-trained intelligence that learns from this archive: soil, water, and the needs of each plant. Modified robotics equipped with nutrient backpacks and precision injection fingers then deliver exact biological care to each root system. The result is abundant, organic, completely free food across the Roaring Fork Valley, eliminating grocery costs and liberating human time for art, connection, and creative purpose.

Philosophy 01
Beyond Categories of Objects

Art as Practice

Art is not just paintings, songs, sculptures, or performances. It is a way of practicing life:

  • A runner refines movement.
  • A carpenter refines precision.
  • A gardener learns timing and relationship with the living world.
  • A cook transforms nourishment into hospitality.
  • A meditator practices attention.
  • An organizer practices bringing people together.

Art is anything practiced with attention, imagination, and the desire to grow. The temples do not divide people into artists and non-artists: they remind us that every life contains disciplines through which attention becomes skill, skill becomes expression, and expression becomes contribution.

Attention to Skill Skill to Expression Daily Life Mastery Creative Contribution
Philosophy 02
Discovery & Celebration

The Return of Play

Modern civilization often treats play as preparation for serious life, expecting adults to surrender playfulness for productivity. Yet play is one of the primary ways human beings discover.

Through play, children test possibilities, adults improvise, artists experiment, and communities laugh together. A peaceful civilization should seek not only to reduce suffering, but to expand the conditions in which people can become playful again: music spilling into shared spaces, dances, games, shared meals, and spontaneous celebrations.

The goal is not constant entertainment: it is the restoration of a social life in which joy is created together rather than consumed alone.

Spontaneous Play Collaborative Joy Unpressured Discovery Shared Celebrations

The Purpose of Automation

Freedom Made Tangible

The promise of technology is often described in terms of speed and efficiency. The Peace Protocol asks what that efficiency is ultimately for. If automation saves people time only so they can consume more, its achievement remains incomplete. If it creates time for learning, health, relationships, play, service, and creative practice, then freedom becomes tangible.

Jedi with a G · Old Snowmass
General Equilibrium Development Institute

The GEDI Training Course

People hear Jedi. We mean GEDI: the General Equilibrium Development Institute. This is the sanctuary ropes course, rebuilt as a training path that brings a person into general equilibrium: body, attention, emotion, and purpose moving as one.

Canopy bridges, creek crossings, rope swings, climbing nets, and balance lines become daily practice in flow and in the zone. Breath, rhythm, and attention move with the body until the nervous system settles. The purpose is better health and wellness, emotional and physical. Beginners are welcomed. Artists, athletes, children, and elders share the same forest lines.

Ambidexterity training is a key element of this design: both hands and both feet, trained as one intelligence. Rings, ropes, nets, and creek lines ask the left side to lead as often as the right, and the feet to think as clearly as the hands.

That equilibrium is not for the self alone. GEDI trains people as samurai in the older sense: skill offered in service to the community around them. You leave the canopy more able to help, build, carry, and protect the people you live among.

Flow & Zone Training

Daily lines through canopy and creek that train presence, breath, and the quiet focus of being in the zone.

Emotional & Physical Wellness

Harmonizing individuals through movement that strengthens the body while settling fear, scatter, and isolation.

Ambidexterity Training

A core element: both hands and both feet trained together, so left and right, grip and step, become one fluent practice.

Samurai Service

The vow at the end of the line: trained people who carry their skill back to the community, not only back to themselves.

Machines Spot. Humans Train as GEDI.

Robotics can watch the line, catch a fall, and keep the course safe. The training itself stays human: breath, both hands, both feet, rhythm, and the courage to serve. GEDI belongs to the Temples of Practice as Movement and Awareness made physical among the trees, then carried home as service.
