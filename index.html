ABOUT DANIEL KELLY BROWN

Part Cinematographer. Part Ninja.

Daniel Kelly Brown is a cinematographer and artist based out of Colorado. Having spent over two decades honing his craft, Danny seamlessly blends technical mastery of robotics and 3D rendering with the raw instinct of an action sports filmmaker.

"Efficiency of movement in life, and during video shoots. I practice and practice to ensure the client gets the perfect shot, every time."

Whether skiing at high speeds with a custom gimbal setup, setting up multiple motion-controlled 8K timelapses on mountain peaks, or flying tight FPV lines through architecture, Danny operates as a complete one-man band.
